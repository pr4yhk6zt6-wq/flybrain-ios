# STATUS — the brief, item by item

The brief is 28 numbered points plus a ten-item instruction to inspect before
changing anything. This file is the ledger: for each item, what exists, which
file owns it, and what is missing. It is written to be checked, not to be
believed — every number here is printed by a tool in this repository, and the
command that prints it is named.

Last updated at `e6d8fb2` (the three commits that fixed the build, the part
axis and the floating windows).

---

## Before modifying code (the ten-item checklist)

1. **Inspect the existing architecture.** Done in steps 1–5; the layout is in
   `README.md`, and each step's own document is in `docs/STEP*.md`.
2. **What already exists.** Body, nerve structure, physics, a live solver on the
   phone, a GPU connectome, a viewer, and this ledger.
3. **Do not duplicate.** `tools/step1_anatomy.py` is the one inventory;
   `tools/build_banc.py` is the one connectome packer; `pack_body.py` and
   `pack_world.py` are the two things that put assets into the app.
4. **Scripted behaviour that violates the rule.** One thing is left, and it is
   labelled: the *recording* in `world/` (a viewer's playback, and since
   `b20c200` no longer shipped inside the app). The stance servo in
   `FlyLiveBody` is declared in `docs/STEP5.md` as a test harness.
5. **Missing links in the closed loop.** Exactly one, and it is step 6:
   `FlyLiveBody.drive` is the hook, `SimulationEngine`'s motor-neuron groups are
   the source, and nothing connects them yet. See §27 below.
6. **Implementation order.** The repository's own steps, which follow the
   brief's §25: 1 body, 2 reflex, 3 loop, 4 six legs, 5 live physics, 6 cord
   into the body, 7 walking, 8 senses, 9 environment, 10 wings/halteres,
   11 resolution, 12 benchmarks on device.
7. **Implement incrementally.** Every commit is one measured step with its
   report regenerated by CI.
8. **Tests after each stage.** `.github/workflows/ios.yml` re-runs steps 1–5
   and the Swift test suite on every push; the numbers in the reports come from
   that run, not from a local machine.
9. **Review for biological shortcuts.** `docs/ASSUMPTIONS.md` is the list: 23
   numbered assumptions, each with the test that would catch it. Nothing is
   "it looked better".
10. **Fix architecture before polish.** This is why the last three commits are a
    build fix, a geometry fix and a layout fix, in that order.

## The 28 points

| # | what the brief asks | where it stands |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | no scripted enemies/food/hunger, only the loop | The app has no behaviour code at all. The Body screen runs `FlyDynamics` (Featherstone ABA) under muscle tone; the only script line in the tree is in `tools/step4_world.py`, which is a measurement tool, not the app. |
| 2 | *D. melanogaster*, adult male, real proportions | `tools/step1_anatomy.py`: 67 parts, **0.985 mg** and **2.97 mm** trunk, read out of the flybody MJCF (a real fly is ~0.96 mg, 2.5–3 mm). Sex: the body model is the published model; BANC is one **female** fly — the mismatch is stated, not hidden. |
| 3 | priority order, graphics last | Honoured: the app's renderer is a Lambert material and three lights. `docs/STEP5.md` spends its length on the solver and none on looks. |
| 4 | low-poly but anatomically structured | The model is the scanned Janelia/DeepMind *flybody*: **85 meshes, 262,180 triangles**, with head, eyes, antennae, proboscis, three thoracic segments, wings, halteres, abdomen and six legs — all present as named geoms. It is heavier than "low poly" and was kept because it is *real*. |
| 5 | leg segments, mass, joints, limits, contact | 6 legs × (coxa, trochanter, femur, tibia, tarsus, tarsomeres, claws) in the MJCF: **102 joints, 102 DOF, 78 actuators** including 8 adhesion actuators on the labrum and claws. `/world` says so in the HUD. |
| 6 | wings: joints, angle, velocity, beat state, aero | Wing meshes, wing joints and 60 wing motor neurons (24 power, 24 steering, 12 tension) exist in the data. The wing *loop* is not wired: brief's §25 step 10. |
| 7 | halteres as sensors | Meshes and 25 haltere motor neurons / 428 haltere sensory cells exist in the connectome. The mechanosensory→motor path is not implemented. Step 10. |
| 8 | multiple connectome resolutions, no fake synapses | **One resolution is real and shipped**: BANC v888, **175,237 neurons, 2,171,713 edges**, 19 MiB packed as CSR (`csrRowPtr/csrColIdx/csrWeight/csrDelay`), integer-atomic LIF on the GPU (`LIF.metal`). No synapses are invented. A resolution *switch* (2M/10M/50M/100M+) does not exist and is not claimed. |
| 9 | brain and VNC separate, nothing bypasses the cord | BANC has both in one animal: 1,316 descending, 2,366 ascending, 12,866 VNC intrinsic. On device they are one network with named groups; the brain→cord→motor ordering is step 6. |
| 10 | vision as signals, not booleans | `CameraFeed` → Metal texture → `sampleRetina` kernel → per-cell luminance with per-cell adaptation, indexed by `retinaUV` for **1,372 left + 1,412 right** retina cells. No motion/optic-flow stage yet. |
| 11 | odour as a field, with wind | **Not implemented.** 3,000 olfactory receptor neurons exist in the connectome and have no stimulus. |
| 12 | touch/mechanosensation from physics | Contact wrenches are computed per geom (`floorContact()`, 74 collision geoms) and summed per leg into `fly.footForce`. Not yet delivered into the neural simulation. |
| 13 | proprioception into the nervous system | `FlyProprioception` (joint angle and rate, per-leg load, height, speed, feet down) is the interface, built from the solver's own state. This is the exact type step 6 hands to `drive`. |
| 14 | motor: neurons → muscles → force, never positions | `FlyDynamics.muscleTorque` is force-based: a Hill force-length curve whose width is the joint's own range, plus a per-direction velocity term. There is no position servo in the app except the declared start-up harness. |
| 15 | muscle model with activation/force/fatigue hooks | Activation, force, length dependence, velocity dependence, damping and armature exist. Fatigue and series elasticity do not, and are listed as not modelled in `docs/ASSUMPTIONS.md`. |
| 16 | physics is the authority | The solver is checked against MuJoCo in CI every run: forward kinematics to **3.3e-16 cm**, 20 ms of free fall with the same 100 µs integrator, and 3 s of the animal standing on muscles alone. |
| 17 | walking emerges from the cord | **Not yet.** Six independent cords (one per leg, 36k–41k neurons each) cannot coordinate, and `docs/STEP4.md` says so: the animal stands and does not walk. This is the next step. |
| 18 | a minimal environment | Floor, grid and three lights. No walls, objects, food, odour or wind yet. |
| 19 | emergent behaviour list | Only **standing** exists, and it is emergent in the strict sense (tone + floor + mass + contacts). |
| 20 | iPhone performance first | Sparse CSR buffers, Metal compute, integer-atomic LIF for A12-class GPUs, no per-neuron Swift objects, and the solver on the display link with a measured real-time factor on the HUD. |
| 21 | graphics budget | Lambert + three directional/ambient lights, no shadows, no post. |
| 22 | observability | The brain screen has Voltage / Spiking / Regions modes, a live HUD (FPS, spikes/s, active, rate, simulated time, power), a neuron inspector with a Stimulate button, and per-region toggles. The Body screen reports feet down, centre of mass, contacts and joint count. Not yet present: per-muscle activation and per-joint torque views. |
| 23 | biological data policy | `docs/ASSUMPTIONS.md`, 23 entries, each with the test that catches it. Two public sources only: flybody (Apache-2.0) and BANC v888 (CC BY 4.0). |
| 24 | modular architecture, no renderer in the nervous system | `Connectome` / `SimulationEngine` (Metal) are independent of `FlyWorld` / `Renderer`; the neural simulation runs headless (`tools/verify_banc.py`). |
| 25 | incremental development | The repository's steps 1–5 are the brief's phases 1–5. |
| 26 | no fake intelligence, no LLM, no RL, no behaviour tree | None of these exist anywhere in the tree. |
| 27 | the acceptance test: the whole loop | Closed **offline** (step 3 in Python: cord → muscle → joint → organ → cord, with a sign-flip control). On device the loop is open at one link: `FlyLiveBody.drive` is unset. That is step 6, and it is the next thing this project does. |
| 28 | judged on fidelity, not looks | That is how every step document is written, including the results that failed. |

## What the last three commits fixed, and why the phone was showing a video

* `7b05f8b` — **the build.** `Build for the simulator` had failed on every run
  since step 5 landed (`FlyDynamics.swift:1024`, a nested `SMat6 * (SVec6 *
  Double)` the type checker refuses). Runs 41 and 43 both died there, so the
  last `.ipa` that finished is run 40 — from before step 5 existed, when the Map
  screen still played `frames.bin` back. **That is the video. It is a build
  artefact, not a feature.**
* `3047e92` — **the geometry.** The recorded pose track has 86 rows per frame and
  row 0 is the floor; both renderers skipped the floor and indexed with their own
  counter, so every mesh wore the pose of the part before it — the scattered
  specks in the Map screen. The manifest now carries `part` per geom and both
  renderers use it. The same commit applies the mesh scale (×0.06) on the live
  path, which had never been applied, so the solver-driven animal would have been
  drawn twenty times life size.
* `b20c200` — **the recording.** The app no longer carries `frames.bin`
  (`NEEDED = world.json, fly.bin`), and CI now *fails* if the `.ipa` contains
  one: "The .ipa must contain its data, and must not contain a recording".
* `e6d8fb2` — **the layout.** One measured `PaneBand` for both screens
  (`LayoutBand.swift`), bars that report their own heights, and a control panel
  that is a list of sections inside a capped height. Nothing overlaps anything,
  on any screen size, by construction.
