What you are watching. The body is the real flybody model (Janelia / Google DeepMind, Vaxenburg et al.). The movement is not animated: every millisecond, each leg's sense organs are told where the joint is and how much load the leg carries, a nerve cord taken out of the Drosophila connectome runs, and its motor pools command the joints. No clock, no gait, no target.
